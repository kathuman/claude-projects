How to play

Players take turns placing a disc of their colour. A move is legal only if it traps one or more of the opponent's discs in a straight line (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) between the disc you just placed and another disc of your colour — every trapped disc then flips. Black moves first. If you have no legal move your turn is skipped; if neither player can move the game ends. Whoever has more discs when the board fills (or both sides are stuck) wins.

Corners can never be flipped, so they are gold; the squares diagonally next to a corner hand it over and are usually poison. The computer knows this.
