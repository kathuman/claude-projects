From one site to a network

With two regions the choice is no longer only when and how much, but where. One central hub pools both regions' demand; regional hubs are close to their customers; and opening one hub creates a cheaper option on the next, because systems, people and supplier terms carry over. Here two regions with correlated, uncertain demand are valued together.

PoolingCentral hub

One hub serves both regions; its spare capacity covers whichever region is busy. Worth most when the regions' demands move independently, and when shipping between them is cheap.

Compound optionPilot hub

Open one region's hub now and keep the option to open the other later, at a lower cost because the first hub exists.

DeferralWait & see network

Serve both regions through 3PLs and open either hub, or both, at the best moment inside the decision window.

The model

Two regions, money in $M. The equations run in src/network.js, which is checked by its own automated tests, including an exact reduction to the single-site engine when one region is empty.

Joint demand lattice

each region: u = e^(σ√Δt), p = (e^(gΔt) − d)/(u − d) p_uu = p_A·p_B + c p_ud = p_A(1−p_B) − c p_du = (1−p_A)p_B − c p_dd = (1−p_A)(1−p_B) + c c = ρ·√(p_A(1−p_A)p_B(1−p_B))

Four branches per quarter. Each region's growth is reproduced exactly and the moves have correlation ρ; if a growth or a correlation cannot be represented, the page says what is used instead.

Serving the network

own_r = min(D_r, K_r) cross = spare capacity → the other region's shortfall 3PL_r = min(rest_r, outCap·D_r,0) CF = margin·own + (margin − x)·cross + outMargin·3PL − penalty·lost − opcost·(K_A + K_B)

Cross-region service earns the margin less the transport cost x, and is used before the 3PL when it earns more.

Compound option

second hub: (F·(1 − learn) + v·K)·(1+e)^t V_A(t) = max(hold, commit(A → AB) − cost_B) V_0(t) = max(wait, → A, → B, → AB)

With one hub open, the option to open the other; with none, the choice of which to open first, or both. Each project arrives after the build lead time; during it no other project starts.

Fixed designs

fixed(K_A, K_B) = Σ_t e^(−rt) E[CF_t]·Δt − cost central = max over K of fixed(K, 0) two = max over K_A, K_B of fixed(K_A, K_B)

The best fixed network is the better of a central hub and two regional hubs, each optimally sized; the value of flexibility is measured against it.
