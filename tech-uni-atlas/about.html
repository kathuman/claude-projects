About the app

Tech University Atlas

Where are the world's technical universities, and how do they rank? This atlas puts the 242 technical universities in the QS World University Rankings 2027 on a spinning globe and a flat map, coloured by rank, so you can see at a glance where engineering education is strongest and how it spreads across 68 countries.

Open the atlas →

What it does

  • Shows them on a globe or a mapThe 3D globe spins and raises a column at each university, taller for a better rank. The flat map uses the Equal Earth projection, which keeps every country at its true size. Drag, scroll to zoom, and click any point.
  • Filters themBy rank tier, by why a university counts as technical, by country, or by typing part of a name, such as Delft, Monterrey, ETH or IIT. The counts and the list follow every filter.
  • Tells you about each oneClick a point or a row in the list: its name, country, QS 2027 rank, where it sits, whether that spot is the main campus or only the city, and why it is on the list.
  • Light or darkDark by default, like the rest of the AI Projects site; the sun button switches to a light map and remembers your choice.

Colours: the rank tiers

Each point is coloured by its QS 2027 rank. A rank published as a band, such as 601–610, is placed by its first number.

  • Top 10016

The five colours were checked to stay apart for the most common kinds of colour blindness, and the list beside the map gives every rank as text.

Which universities count as technical?

The rankings do not label universities as technical, so the atlas uses three simple tests. A university is included if it passes any of them; the shape of its point shows which one.

Its English name contains “tech” 186Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Delft University of Technology, Georgia Tech. Shown as a plain dot.
Its name contains “tec”, accents ignored 14Names in Spanish, Portuguese or Italian, such as Tecnológico de Monterrey or Politecnico di Milano. Shown with a ring.
It is technical without saying so in English 42Its name in its own language means technical university or polytechnic (ETH Zurich, TU Wien, RWTH Aachen); it belongs to a national network of technical universities (TU9, TU Austria, 4TU, Nordic Five Tech, the Malaysian Technical University Network); or it teaches only one engineering field, such as mining, petroleum, aerospace or telecoms. Shown as a square.

Where the data comes from

Ranks: the QS World University Rankings 2027, published on 18 June 2026, which rank 1,504 institutions. The atlas keeps each rank exactly as published. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by QS.

Locations: the coordinates were compiled by Claude, an AI model, not looked up by a mapping service. 210 points sit on the main campus; for 32 only the city was certain, and those are marked “city-level” in the list. A point can be off by a few streets, so check a precise location before relying on it.

Country outlines: Natural Earth, simplified to about 50 metres. The globe is drawn with globe.gl.
