Legend

Colour shows the QS rank tier. A ring marks a “tec” name match (Tecnológico, Politécnico). A square marks a technical university without tech/tec in its name, such as ETH Zurich, TU Wien or RWTH Aachen. On the globe, column height rises with rank.

Technical status means the native name designates a technical university or polytechnic, the institution belongs to a national technical-university network (TU9, TU Austria, 4TU, Nordic Five Tech, Malaysian Technical University Network), or it is a single-field engineering, mining, petroleum, aerospace or telecom university.

Points sit on the main campus. Rows marked “city-level” in the list are placed at city precision.
