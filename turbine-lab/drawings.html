How to read the drawing
  • Each machine is cut through the centre of its runner along three planes. Section A–A is cut from front to back, B–B from side to side and C–C horizontally; the isometric view shows the whole machine.
  • The chain lines with letters show where the other sections are cut; the arrows show which way you look at the cut.
  • Hatched areas are solid material that the cutting plane passes through. Thin-walled parts (casings, ducts) are shown by their thick cut outline.
  • The views are in first-angle projection (the European convention): the view from the left is drawn to the right of the front view, and the view from above is drawn below it.
  • The numbered balloons match the parts list. Dimensions are overall sizes of the model in millimetres. Click a view to open it at full size.
