A project keeps the picture as the solver saw it, not the original photo. To change the crop, the adjustments or the brush marks, upload the photo again.

Choose a picture: a face or object with strong contrast against a plain background works best. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; everything runs in your browser.

Importance brush

Paint on the Target view. Warm areas count up to three times as much and draw more lines; cool areas count as little as a fifth. Use it for eyes, a mouth or an edge that matters.

Parameters

Nail template

The nail positions at true size, numbered, to tape to the board. Print at actual size (100 %, not “fit to page”) and check the 100 mm square with a ruler.
