Build the winding machine

A turntable carries the nail board; a thread guide above its rim moves only in and out and up and down. Everything here is worked out from one set of dimensions, for a 600 mm nail circle with 240 nails: the 3D model, the printed parts, the drilling plans and the controller settings. The machine page plans the moves and writes the G-code.
