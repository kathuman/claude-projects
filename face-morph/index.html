How this works

Detection. Two small convolutional networks run entirely in your browser via TensorFlow.js (face-api.js): a TinyFaceDetector finds the face box, then a 68-point landmark network locates the jaw, eyebrows, eyes, nose and mouth within it. Both model files together are under 600 KB and are vendored in this repo — no CDN, no API call, no network request carries your image anywhere.

Capture. A capture is a 480×480 snapshot (from your camera or an uploaded photo) plus the 68 landmark points detected on it, with 8 extra points added around the frame's edges so the whole photo warps smoothly, not just an oval cut-out of the face.

Morphing. The 68+8 = 76 points always mean the same thing in the same order for every capture (point 30 is always the nose tip, point 8 is always the chin, and so on), so instead of re-triangulating on the fly, this app uses one fixed Delaunay triangulation — 142 triangles — computed once offline on a canonical face layout and verified to exactly tile the frame with no gaps or overlaps (triangle-area sum matches the convex-hull area to floating-point precision). At blend position t, each triangle's target shape is the linear interpolation between its shape in photo A and photo B; both photos are warped into that shared target shape with a per-triangle affine transform (ctx.transform + clip + drawImage), then cross-dissolved (A drawn opaque, B drawn on top at alpha t). That's the whole algorithm — the same one behind classic photo-morph effects, just computed live in a <canvas>.

  • Works best with a clear, front-facing, evenly lit face.
  • Only one face is detected per capture — for a group photo, crop to one person first.
  • Captures live only in this browser tab's memory — reload the page and they're gone. Nothing is written to disk or sent anywhere unless you click Download Frame.
