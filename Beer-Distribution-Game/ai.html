AI players

The chain played by AIs. Every stage is run by its own AI (Claude Opus 5.5), deciding its order each week on its own, without talking to the others. The game is played twice: once with each AI seeing only its own stage, once with each seeing the whole chain. Then the two are compared. Every AI is told the game never ends.
