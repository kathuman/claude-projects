Ripple

A chain of companies brings one product from a plant to a store. Each orders from the one above it, and every order and shipment takes time to arrive. Your team runs the chain on this device, each stage seeing only its own numbers. The goal: the lowest total cost for the whole team, counting stock you hold and orders you cannot fill.
