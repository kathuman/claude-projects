Your cube

Paint each sticker as it is on your cube: pick a colour, then click the stickers. Hold the cube with the white centre up and the green centre facing you; the net shows each face as you look straight at it. Or let the camera read it.

The picture stays on this device: nine colours are read from it, nothing is sent anywhere.
