The cycle on the refrigerant's pressure–enthalpy chart. Inside the dome the refrigerant boils or condenses at constant temperature; the horizontal lengths are the energy per kilogram: heat taken from the source (4→1), compressor work (1→2) and heat delivered (2→3).

The same cycle on the temperature–entropy chart, with the heat source and the heating circuit. The gaps between the refrigerant and the water or air are what the heat exchangers need to work; every kelvin of gap costs COP.

Temperatures along each heat exchanger, against the heat transferred. The refrigerant must stay hotter than the water in the condenser and colder than the source in the evaporator everywhere — the smallest gap is the pinch.
